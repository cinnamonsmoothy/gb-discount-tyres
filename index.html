How do I know which tyre size I need?+

Check the size printed on your tyre sidewall. A registration lookup identifies the vehicle but cannot confirm its fitted tyre size.

Can you repair a puncture?+

We offer puncture repairs. Whether a particular tyre can be repaired depends on the location and extent of the damage. Bring it in and we can inspect it.

Do you offer MOTs and servicing?+

Yes. MOT testing, servicing and maintenance are among the services listed here. Call the team to discuss what your vehicle needs and arrange a visit.

Are the tyre prices and stock shown live?+

No. The tyre options and prices on this site are examples. Call us for current stock, an exact fit and a quote.

Find us at 158 Bedfont Lane, Feltham TW14 9NJ, a few doors from Great Wall Fish & Chips and Bedfont Food & Wine. See the map and directions.
